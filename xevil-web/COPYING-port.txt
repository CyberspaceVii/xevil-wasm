XEvil WebAssembly port for cyberspace7.org
========================================

This directory (xevil-web/) holds the port of XEvil 2.1 beta to the browser:
an Xlib-on-SDL2 shim (xshim.cpp + xinc/X11/*.h), a frame-driven main
(xevil_main.cpp), the emscripten glue (glue.js) and the build scripts.

Rebuild: ./build-wasm.sh  (emscripten; output out-wasm/xevil.{js,wasm})
         ./build-native-sdl.sh  (g++ + SDL2; out-native/xevil-sdl)
Upstream files patched at build time are listed in patch-sources.sh.
